What we offer

Our Services

African Foods Company markets and export South African canned, vacuum packed, bottled or in sealed packaging Food to people living overseas who miss the taste of home and struggle to find it in stores.

Services We Provide

Canned Foods

Various canned foods such as KOO Beans, KOO Jam & Fruit and Chakalaka available for export to overseas markets.

Vacuum Sealed Foods

Products such as vacuum-sealed meats (Biltong) and dried fruits that provide extended shelf life.

Bottled Packaged Foods

Various sauces (Mrs. H.S Balls Chutney, All Gold Tomato Sauce), beverages (Ginger Beer,)and preserved Miami Gherkins & Pickled Onions and Pickles packed in bottles for convenience and longevity.

Boxed & Bagged pantry Foods

Products such as Ouma rusks, Fatti's & Moni's Pasta, White star Samp & Beans, Maize Meal, Jungle Oats, Bokomo Weet-Bix are sold by AFC.
